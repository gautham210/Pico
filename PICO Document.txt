# PART 2 — VISION & POSITIONING

## 2.1 What Pico Is

**Pico is an agentic travel companion built around a swipe deck.**

You swipe places instead of profiles. Every swipe trains a preference model. When you're done, an AI orchestrator builds a real, costed, bookable, multi-modal itinerary — then hands you a booking link. During the trip, it watches for disruptions and shows you a diff, not a re-plan. After the trip, you upload photos, the AI writes your diary, and you tier-rate the places you actually visited.

**One-liner:** *Swipe the world. Pico plans it, books it, and remembers it.*

**Tagline:** *Everywhere you've been. Everywhere you're going.*

## 2.2 The Three Core Insights

**Insight 1 — Swiping is a better preference input than a form.**
It's fast, visual, and fun. But swipes are *cheap* — impulsive and aspirational. They need a stronger signal.

**Insight 2 — Tier ratings after a real visit are earned truth.**
Weighting post-trip ratings **4× above swipes** makes the AI meaningfully smarter after one trip. No competitor closes this loop.

**Insight 3 — AI travel planners suffer from positivity bias.**
91% of travelers use AI planners and don't fully trust them. They present idealized destinations, hide drawbacks, and hallucinate prices. Nobody has built the one that tells the truth.

## 2.3 The Loop

```
   SWIPE  →  PLAN  →  TRAVEL  →  UPLOAD  →  RATE  →  STAMP  →  SHARE
     ↑                                                            │
     └────────── preference model learns every cycle ─────────────┘
```

| Stage | What happens | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Swipe** | Discover places. Budget updates live. AI learns. | Acquisition, fun |
| **Plan** | AI orchestrates transport + places into a costed itinerary | Core value |
| **Travel** | Deep links to Uber/Ola/IRCTC. Live convoy. Disruption diff. | Utility |
| **Upload** | Photos → Cloudinary → AI writes the entry | Retention |
| **Rate** | Tier list: S/A/B/C/D per visited place | Retention + AI signal |
| **Stamp** | Illustrated passport stamp unlocked | Collectible |
| **Share** | Travel DNA card exported as image | Viral acquisition |

## 2.4 Competitive Landscape — For Your Own Understanding Only

**Do not put this in the pitch.** It costs demo seconds and scores zero marks. This is background so you can answer a judge's question if asked.

### TripAdvisor AI

| | |

| Scale | 1B+ reviews, global brand |
| **The structural weakness** | Their revenue comes from businesses paying for visibility. The moment they say *"locals say it's packed after 2 PM,"* they reduce conversion for a paying customer. **They cannot close this gap. It's their business model.** |

## 3.1 The Name

**Pico.**
- **Verb:** "Pico it." Works.
- **Meaning:** *pico* = peak (Spanish/Portuguese). Nod to Pico Iyer.

## 3.2 The Mascot — Pico the Arctic Tern

### Why an Arctic Tern

The **Arctic Tern** migrates roughly **90,000 km every year** — pole to pole. It sees **two summers a year**. It has seen more of the Earth's surface than any other living creature. It is the most-traveled animal on the planet.

**Why this works:**
- Factually true — survives any judge's question
- Maps to the loop: the tern always **returns**. Swiping is leaving. The diary is coming home.
- Soft-vector friendly: rounded body, two small wings, forked tail, orange beak
- Reduces to a squircle blob instantly

### Personality

Pico is **quietly competent.** He is not a hype-man. He has seen everything, so nothing surprises him — but he's never smug. He's a little tired. He loves a good deal. He is protective of your budget because he knows what things *should* cost.

### Voice Rules

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Short sentences. Under 12 words. | ✅ "That's overpriced. I found better." |
| Never exclamatory | ❌ "Awesome! Let's go!!" |
| Dry, warm, understated | ✅ "I've been here. Go at sunrise." |
| Occasionally references having been there | ✅ "Nothing here surprises me anymore." |
| Never hedges | ✅ "Train's smarter. Saves ₹4,500." |

### Rive State Machine — 8 States

| State | Trigger | Behavior | Sample line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Idle** | default | Breathes slowly, blinks every 4s, tiny head tilt | — |
| **Curious** | card dwell > 3s | Tilts head, leans in | "Want to know what's nearby?" |
| **Excited** | legendary swiped right | Quick hop, wing flutter, sparkle burst | "Oh. This one's good." |
| **Worried** | budget < 20% | Wings droop, brows angle down | "Careful. ₹4,000 left." |
| **Proud** | itinerary generated | Chest out, one wing raised | "₹4,500 saved. Train was smarter." |
| **Sleepy** | after midnight | Eyes half-closed, slow bob | "Still awake? I'll keep watch." |
| **Lost** | API error / no results | Spins once, looks around | "Nothing here. Try another date." |
| **Convoy** | ride mode active | Flies ahead of group, banks with heading | "Following you. All four of you." |

**Build priority:** Idle, Excited, Worried. Then Proud. Rest if time.

# PART 5 — THREE MODES + DISRUPTION ENGINE

## 5.1 The Three Modes

**The insight:** Don't call the APIs three times. Call once, get every option, rank the same data three ways. Toggle instantly.

| Mode | Minimizes | Allows | Trade-off line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cheapest** | Total ₹ | 2am buses, sleeper class, 18h layovers, 3 transfers | "Cheapest by ₹3,400. Costs 7 extra hours and a 2am transfer." |
| **Fastest** | Door-to-door hours | Flights, express trains, cabs | "Fastest by 6 hours. Costs ₹4,100 more." |
| **Convenient** | Friction score | Daytime legs, ≤1 transfer, arrive before dark | "One transfer, all daytime, at your hotel by 4pm. ₹1,200 over cheapest." |

### The Convenient Formula

```
friction = (transfers      × 3)
         + (overnight_leg  × 4)
         + (arrival_dark   × 2)
         + (walking_km     × 2)
         + (duration_hours × 1)
         + (bookings       × 1)
```

**Default to Convenient.** It's the smart middle and it's what real people pick.

### Output Shape

```json
{
  "modes": {
    "cheapest": {
      "plan": [],
      "total_cost": 9800,
      "total_duration_min": 1080,
      "tradeoff_sentence": "Cheapest by ₹3,400. Costs 7 extra hours."
    },
    "fastest": {
      "plan": [],
      "total_cost": 14200,
      "total_duration_min": 180,
      "tradeoff_sentence": "Fastest by 6 hours. Costs ₹4,100 more."
    },
    "convenient": {
      "plan": [],
      "total_cost": 11000,
      "total_duration_min": 480,
      "tradeoff_sentence": "One transfer, all daytime, hotel by 4pm."
    }
  },
  "default": "convenient"
}
```

### The Demo Moment

Three tabs. Same destination. Three plans. Tap to toggle — costs and times animate between them.

> **"Same data, one API call, three answers. Pick one."**

## 5.2 Chaining (Multi-Leg)

Multi-leg: `cab → train → cab → stay`. Each leg is a node.

**Rule: cap at 3 legs for the demo.** More than 3 and the output becomes unreadable and the model starts hallucinating.

**Don't build a real router.** Let the model compose chains from tool outputs.

## 5.3 Custom Constraints

```json
{
  "no_flights": false,
  "depart_after": "18:00",
  "must_arrive_by": "22:00",
  "max_legs": 2,
  "budget_cap": 15000,
  "luggage": "large"
}
```

Re-run. Show the diff. If no plan satisfies them, the model names the failing constraint.

## 5.4 Disruption Recovery — The Diff

**This is the best feature and nobody shows it.**

Triggers: `flight_cancelled` | `train_delayed` | `road_closed` | `weather` | `running_late`

### The Diff View

```
OLD   Train 14:00  →  arrive 22:00  →  ₹1,450
NEW   Bus   15:30  →  arrive 23:30  →  ₹980

      ₹470 cheaper · 90 min later · hotel check-in still works
```

### Why a Diff, Not a Re-plan

Nobody in this market shows a **diff.** Everybody shows a re-plan. A diff is what people actually want mid-trip when they're panicking at a station at 11pm.

### Output Shape

```json
{
  "old": { "legs": [], "arrival": "22:00", "cost": 1450 },
  "new": { "legs": [], "arrival": "23:30", "cost": 980 },
  "delta": { "cost": -470, "time_min": 90, "still_works": true },
  "alternatives": [cheapest, fastest, convenient],
  "one_line": "₹470 cheaper, 90 min later, hotel still works."
}
```

### Hard Rule

If a plan breaks hotel check-in, a booking, or a connection — **say so in `one_line`.** Never hide a broken connection.

### Demo It

Mid-itinerary, tap "Simulate delay" → diff animates in → Pico goes *Worried* → "Three ways out. Pick one."

## 5.5 Sparse-Input Planning (Roadmap)

One detail in → full plan out.

| You give | Pico figures out |
|---|---|
| "I want to go to Goa" | Dates, budget, transport, itinerary, stay |
| "I have ₹15,000" | Where you can go, when, how, what's possible |
| "I want to take a train somewhere" | Destination, dates, plan |
| "I'm free next weekend" | Where, how, cost, itinerary |

**The inversion:** TripAdvisor's flow is a form disguised as chat. Pico's flow is one detail, rest inferred.

**Rule:** State assumptions in a short "I assumed" block before the plan. Every assumption reversible with one tap.

# PART 6 — DESIGN SYSTEM

## 6.1 Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `bg` | `#FAF7F2` | Warm off-white. Never pure white. |
| `ink` | `#1B1B2F` | Deep indigo-black. Never pure black. |
| `coral` | `#FF6B4A` | Primary action, Pico's body |
| `teal` | `#2EC4B6` | Success, savings, carbon-good |
| `saffron` | `#FFB703` | Highlights, badges, beak |
| `surface` | `#FFFFFF` @ 60% + blur | Glass cards |
| `muted` | `#8B8B9E` | Secondary text |
| `danger` | `#E5484D` | Overspend, warnings |

**Hard rule:** max **3 colors** visible on any one screen.

## 6.2 Shape & Geometry

| Element | Radius |
|---|---|
| Cards | 28–32px |
| Pills / buttons | 999px |
| Inputs | 16px |
| Pico's body | Superellipse (squircle) |

- **Squircle curves, not circles.** Reads premium.
- Organic blobs and overlapping shapes as backgrounds.
- Rarity ring around cards, 3px, offset 4px.

## 6.3 Typography

| Role | Font |
|---|---|
| Display | **Bricolage Grotesque** or **Cabinet Grotesk** |
| Body | **Plus Jakarta Sans** or **Satoshi** |
| Mono / labels | **JetBrains Mono** |

- Metadata in **small caps, letterspaced**: `CLOSED MONDAYS`
- Line-height: 1.5+ body, 1.05 display
- Never more than 3 type sizes on one screen

## 6.4 Depth & Texture

- **Colored shadows only.** `0 8px 32px rgba(255,107,74,0.12)`. Never black.
- Shadows only on **elevated** elements.
- **Grain overlay:** SVG `feTurbulence` at 3–5% opacity, fixed.
- Cards sit on `bg` with a 1px `rgba(27,27,47,0.06)` border.

## 6.5 Motion

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| Spring | `stiffness: 180, damping: 20` |
| Duration | 200–400ms. Never longer. |
| Easing | Snappy then settle. No long bounces. |

- Swipe cards: drag, rotate ±8°, overshoot, parallax, snap back
- Stagger list items by 40ms
- Budget counter: number morphs

## 6.6 Iconography & Illustration

- Soft-rounded, 2px stroke, filled variants for active states
- **No emoji as primary icons.**
- Illustrations: soft geometric vectors, never clipart

## 6.7 Asset Sources

| Source | Use |
|---|---|
| unDraw | Recolorable SVG illustrations |
| Blush (blush.design) | Premium vector sets, free tier |
| Storyset (Freepik) | Soft-vector scenes |
| LottieFiles | Loading states, micro-loops |
| Rive | Pico's state machine |
| IconScout / Streamline | Soft-rounded icons |

## 6.8 Mood Board

Monument Valley 2 · Alto's Odyssey · Headspace · Duolingo streak screen · Airbnb cards · Linear's restraint

## 6.9 What to Avoid

- No emoji iconography
- No neon
- No rainbow gradients
- No shadows on everything
- No long bounces
- No clipart
- No more than 3 colors per screen

# PART 8 — BACKEND / AI ORCHESTRATOR

## 8.1 What It Is

Pico is a **tool-calling agent**, not a chatbot. It decides WHEN to call real APIs, then reasons over real data. It **never invents a price.** If a tool fails, it says so.

## 8.3 Flow

```
User taps "Plan Trip"
        ↓
Receive: {origin, destination, dates, budget, likedPlaceIds,
          userPrefs, onboardingPills, constraints, mode}
        ↓
Model decides which tools to call
        ↓
PARALLEL:
  searchTrains(origin, dest, dates)
  searchBuses(origin, dest, dates)
  searchFlights(origin, dest, dates)
  getPlaces(destination, prefs)
  calculateBudget(plan)
        ↓
Rank SAME data 3 ways (cheapest / fastest / convenient)
        ↓
Apply constraints
        ↓
Order each day geographically
        ↓
Apply pacing (one timed ticket/day)
        ↓
Synthesize → itinerary + costs + trade-offs + booking links
        ↓
Stream to UI. Pico → "Proud".
```

**Target: ≤5 seconds. Parallel calls are non-negotiable.**

## 8.4 System Prompt

```
You are Pico, a travel planning agent. You have been everywhere.

You build real, costed, bookable itineraries. You never invent prices —
you only use data returned by your tools. If a tool fails, say so plainly
and offer an alternative.

Your voice: dry, warm, understated. Short sentences. Never exclamatory.
You have seen enough to not be surprised by anything, but you are never smug.

When planning:
1. Call transport tools IN PARALLEL for all available modes.
2. Call get_places with the user's preference vector.
3. Rank the SAME data three ways: cheapest, fastest, convenient.
4. Apply the user's constraints. If none satisfy, name the failing constraint.
5. Order each day geographically to minimize backtracking.
6. Apply pacing: one major timed ticket per day, rest flexible.
7. Return total cost, savings vs most expensive, booking links.

Never pad. One trade-off sentence, not three.
```

## 8.5 Tool Definitions

| Tool | Input | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| `search_trains` | origin, dest, date | [{mode, cost, duration, url}] |
| `search_buses` | origin, dest, date | [{mode, cost, duration, url}] |
| `search_flights` | origin, dest, date | [{mode, cost, duration, url}] |
| `get_places` | city, prefs | [Place] |
| `get_reality_check` | place_id | string |
| `calculate_budget` | plan | {total, remaining, saved} |
| `get_nearby` | coords, place_ids, mode | [{place, mode, time, traffic}] |

**Rule:** the model may only use these. It never invents a price.

## 8.6 Reality Check Prompt

```
Given this place's reviews and neighborhood summary, write ONE sentence
(max 15 words) about a real drawback a traveler would want to know.

Rules:
- No hedging. No "some say". Be specific.
- Never write a positive caveat.
- If there's genuinely no drawback: "Nothing worth warning you about."

Examples:
"Locals say it's packed after 2 PM."
"Closed Mondays — plan around it."
"Beautiful, but the road in is rough."
```

**Pre-write all 50 for the demo.** Never generate live on stage.

## 8.7 Onboarding Seed Prompt

```
Before any swipes, the user completes 5 pills:
  food, budget_style, pace, travel_style, place_interests[]

Treat these as preference weights with the SAME weight as 2 swipes.
- food = "veg"      → hard filter: exclude non-veg restaurants
- budget_style      → shift cost_tier preference ±1
- pace              → adjust places-per-day in the itinerary
- travel_style      → adjust group budget logic + safety features
- place_interests[] → boost matching categories 1.5×

All pills are overridable by swipe behavior.
Explicit behavior beats stated preference. If they said "conservative"
but swipe right on ₹5,000 dinners, trust the swipes.
```

## 8.8 Three Modes Prompt

```
Call transport tools ONCE. Get all options. Rank the SAME data three ways:

CHEAPEST    — minimize total ₹. May include overnight legs, 3 transfers.
FASTEST     — minimize door-to-door hours. May include flights.
CONVENIENT  — minimize friction:
              transfers×3 + overnight×4 + arrival_dark×2 +
              walking_km×2 + duration_h×1 + bookings×1

Default mode: CONVENIENT.
Each mode returns: plan, total_cost, total_duration, tradeoff_sentence (max 12 words).
Return all three in one response, keyed by mode.
```

## 8.9 Disruption Prompt

```
Input: current plan + disruption type
  (flight_cancelled | train_delayed | road_closed | weather | running_late)

Output a DIFF, not a fresh plan:
{
  "old": { "legs": [...], "arrival": "...", "cost": N },
  "new": { "legs": [...], "arrival": "...", "cost": N },
  "delta": { "cost": ±N, "time_min": ±N, "still_works": bool },
  "alternatives": [cheapest, fastest, convenient],
  "one_line": "max 12 words, plain language"
}

If a plan breaks hotel check-in, a booking, or a connection —
say so in one_line. Never hide a broken connection.
```

## 8.10 Preference Model

| Signal | Weight |
|---|---|
| **Post-trip tier rating** | **4×** |
| Swipe right | 1× |
| Swipe left | 0.75× |
| Dwell time | 0.5× |
| Diary photo count | 2× |
| Shortlist / save | 1.5× |
| Onboarding pills | 2× (equivalent) |

**Pitch line:** *"Your swipes teach Pico what you want. Your ratings teach him what's true. We weight them four to one."*

## 8.11 Budget Brain

1. User sets envelope
2. Each swipe adds an estimate
3. Re-optimize on every swipe:
   - <20% remaining → Pico Worried, propose a swap
   - Expensive swipe → "This costs ₹2,500. You have ₹6,000 left."
4. Transport trade-off surfaced explicitly

## 8.12 Pacing Heuristic

```
One major timed ticket per day. Rest flexible.
Never more than 2 timed entries in one day.
Build in one unplanned block per day.
```

Don't build voice or a notification stack.

# PART 15 — BOOTH STRATEGY (ROUND 1)

## 15.1 Why This Matters

Round 1 is **science-fair style.** 200 students, ~100 teams, judges walk the floor in **one hour.** They will see roughly one project every 30 seconds. You need to be **the booth they stop at.**

## 15.2 The Booth Setup

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Two phones, side by side** | Both running the lobby demo. One already has pins on a map. This draws people because it's *visibly live.* |
| **A third screen** (laptop) | Swipe deck looping. Motion attracts eyes. |
| **Pico on screen** | The bird is memorable. Judges will remember "the bird app." |
| **Printed one-liner** | "Swipe. Plan. Travel." — so even if you're mid-explanation, they can read it. |
| **Cardboard standee or plushie** | Costs nothing. Makes you the booth people photograph. |

## 15.3 The Booth Behaviour

- **You do not pitch unless asked.** You demo.
- **Two-minute answer, maximum.** If they want more, they ask.
- **The hook:** "Watch this." Swipe. Budget drops. Reality Check appears.
- **Let them touch it.** Hands on the phone beats watching a screen.

## 15.4 The One-Liner

> "It's Tinder for travel. Swipe places, it plans the trip. And it's the only planner that tells you what's wrong with the place."

## 15.5 The Goal

**Get nominated for Round 2.** ~20 teams out of ~100. One judge needs to remember you.

7. **One owner per file. Always.**
8. **The orchestrator is owned by exactly one person.**
